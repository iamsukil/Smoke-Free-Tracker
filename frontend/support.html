💡 When to Call

🆘
Feeling an intense craving
A counsellor can talk you through it and help you resist the urge.
😟
Feeling anxious or irritable
Nicotine withdrawal symptoms peak in days 2–3. Support helps.
🔁
Had a relapse
A slip is not a failure. Call and get back on track today.
🤔
Need a personalised quit plan
Counsellors can recommend NRT options and set milestone goals with you.
